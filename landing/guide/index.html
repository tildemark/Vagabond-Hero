🛡️ Class Progression & Dynamic Secondary Resources

Unlike generic RPGs that lock every archetype into standard Mana, Vagabond Hero provides dynamic resources with unique generation loops, HUD colors, and playstyle requirements.

Vagabond
Resource: Grit (0–100)

Base survivor class (Lv 1–20). Willpower generated by surviving hits (+15) and basic attacks (+5).

Juggernaut
Resource: Fury (0–100)

Melee titan. Builds Fury through combat clash (+10 hit, +15 struck). Decays outside battle.

Phantom
Resource: Energy (0–100)

Agility striker. Starts at 100 with rapid turn regeneration (+25/turn) for lethal multi-hit combos.

Weaver
Resource: Mana (0–Max MP)

Deep arcane reservoir scaled from INT (Base 50 + INT * 5). High spell costs balanced by siphons.

Warden
Resource: Animus (0–100)

Pack master. Generated through summon attacks (+15) and life leeching (+10) to sustain barriers.

📊 Core Attributes & Mathematical Formulas

Vagabond Hero computes character defense, damage mitigation, and utility through clear, deterministic formulas.

Core Attribute Primary Effects Secondary Conversions
Strength (STR) Increases Physical Damage (Swords, Maces, Axes) +1 Base Armor per 2 STR points
Agility (AGI) Increases Finesse/Ranged Damage (Daggers, Bows) +0.1% Evasion & +0.1% Critical Chance per AGI
Intelligence (INT) Increases Magic Damage (Wands, Staffs, Spells) +5 Max Mana per INT point (Weavers) & Elemental Resists
Stamina (STA) Core Survival Attribute +10 Maximum HP per STA point & +1 Armor per 4 STA

Elemental Infusions & Combat Utility Caps

Combat Stat Type Hard Cap Description & Synergy
Elemental Resistances Defensive 75% Fire, Ice, Poison, Void, Lightning, and Water damage reduction.
Critical Strike Chance Offensive 75% Base Critical Hit Damage starts at 150% and scales with gear.
Life Steal Sustain 30% Heals character for a % of actual unmitigated damage dealt.
Damage Reflect (Thorns) Counter Uncapped Deals flat and % reflected physical damage back when struck.
Cooldown Reduction (CDR) Utility 50% Reduces active skill turn cooldowns (rounded to nearest integer).
Magic Find (MF) Loot Pool Uncapped Increases probability that procedural drops roll as Rare or Legendary.

💎 The Gem Forge & Socketing Matrix

Socketable gems drop from Level 15 onward. Sockets vary by slot (up to 3 in Weapons & Torso). Slotting gems grants distinct bonuses depending on whether inserted into Weapons, Armor, or Jewelry.

Gem Type Weapon Slot (Offense) Armor / Head / Legs (Defense) Jewelry Slot (Utility)
Ruby (Fire) + Flat Fire Damage + Flat Max HP + % Fire Resistance
Sapphire (Ice) + Flat Ice Damage + Max Secondary Resource (Grit/Fury/Energy/Mana/Animus) + % Ice Resistance
Emerald (Poison) + Flat Poison Damage + Flat Armor + % Poison Resistance
Amethyst (Void) + Flat Void Damage + % Damage Reflect (Thorns) + % Void Resistance
Diamond (Core) + % Boss Damage + % All Resistances + % Magic Find (Loot Rarity)
Topaz (Wealth) + % Life Steal + % Evasion + % Gold Drop Rate
Pearl (Swiftness) + % Attack Speed + % Movement / Avoidance + % EXP Gained Boost

Tier Combining Formula (The Blacksmith)

Combine 3 Gems of the same tier plus Silver Prisms at any Waypoint Anvil to synthesize the next tier:
Chipped (Lv 1+) ➔ Flawed (Lv 15+) ➔ Regular (Lv 30+) ➔ Flawless (Lv 50+) ➔ Perfect (Lv 70) ➔ Radiant (Act 5 Glitched)

⚡ Glitched Modifiers & The Level 70 Echo Quest

Progression beyond Level 70 is unlocked via the pinnacle quest "Breaking the Core". Completing it grants access to Glitched (Red) item drops and Paragon-style Echo micro-allocation.

Corrupted Item Infusion
Post-Echo Pinnacle Mechanics
System Rule

Once "Breaking the Core" is cleared, all newly dropped Legendary and Set items have a 10% baseline probability to roll a Glitched Modifier on top of their normal modifier budget.

  • Positive Effect: Massive god-tier buffs (+250% skill dmg, double casts, etc.).
  • Negative Penalty: Severe build constraints (-50% Max Mana, zero evasion, HP recoil).
The Echo Point System
Infinite Level 70+ Progression
Paragon

EXP earned after Level 70 fills the Echo Bar. Each Echo Level grants 1 point for micro-optimizations:

  • Offense: +0.2% Crit Damage, +0.1% Attack Speed, +0.2% Elemental Infusion.
  • Defense: +0.2% Armor, +0.1% Life Steal, +0.2% All-Resist.
  • Utility: +0.5% Gold Drop Rate, +0.5% Magic Find, +0.2% Resource Generation.
