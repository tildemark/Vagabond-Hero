🛡️ Class Progression & Skill Codices

Every wanderer begins as an unspecialized Vagabond. Master field survival techniques before choosing your First Job Awakening at Level 20.

The 4-Stage Evolution Path (Lv 1 → 70+ Echo)
Tier 1 • Levels 1–20
🎒 The Vagabond

The universal starting survivor. All players begin here, relying on scrap weapons, emergency triage, and high resourcefulness.

  • Resource: Grit (0–100)
  • Stat Growth: +3 Points/Lv (+1 to All Auto)
  • Milestone: Level 20 Job Choice
Tier 2 • Levels 21–50
⚔️ First Job Awakening

Specialization unlocks into one of four archetypes: Juggernaut (STR), Phantom (AGI), Weaver (INT), or Warden (Hybrid/STA).

  • Resource: Fury / Energy / Mana / Animus
  • Stat Growth: +5 Points/Lv (+1 Core Stat Auto)
  • Milestone: Level 50 Subclass Choice
Tier 3 • Levels 51–70
🔮 Second Job Mastery

Each archetype branches into two advanced subclasses (e.g. Berserker vs. Void-Knight, Assassin vs. Rift-Sniper, Elementalist vs. Blood Mage).

  • Resource: Subclass Evolution Mechanics
  • Stat Growth: +6 Points/Lv (+15 Max HP/Lv)
  • Milestone: Breaking the Core Quest
Tier 4 • Level 70+ Pinnacle
👑 Pinnacle & Echoes

Unlocked post-"Breaking the Core". Unlocks reality-breaking capstone skills, Corrupted (Red) gear permission, and infinite Echo levels.

  • Echo Offense: Crit Chance, Boss Dmg, Crit Dmg, Spd
  • Echo Defense: All Resist, Reflect, Evade, Armor
  • Echo Utility: CDR, Life Steal, Magic Find, Gold
  • Progression: +1 Echo Point per Echo Lv

The Vagabond
Tier 1 Universal Starting Survivor • Levels 1–20 (All Adventurers Start Here)
Resource: Grit (0–100) Primary: All Balanced Armor: Scrap & Rags

Every hero in the Shattered Expanse starts here. Stripped of memories and divine favor, the Vagabond survives purely on grit, scavenged scraps, and raw adaptability. They possess heightened baseline luck to unearth relics and emergency triage techniques to bandage wounds in hostile territory before choosing their awakened path.

Resource Mechanics
Grit (0–100)
Starts at 50. Generates +10/turn, +15 when struck, and +5 on basic strikes. Sustains triage & escape rolls.
Stat Growth per Level
+3 Attribute Points
Automatic growth: +5 Max HP and +1 to All Stats (STR/AGI/INT/STA) automatically per level up.
Evolution Milestone
Level 20 Job Awakening
Upon conquering Act I's trial, awaken into the Juggernaut, Phantom, Weaver, or Warden below.
Tactical Advantage
Survivor Utility
High baseline Gold find, emergency field triage, and trap/terrain damage immunity.

📊 Core Attributes & Mathematical Formulas

Vagabond Hero computes character defense, damage mitigation, and utility through clear, deterministic formulas.

Core Attribute Primary Effects Secondary Conversions
Strength (STR) Increases Physical Damage (Swords, Maces, Axes) +1 Base Armor per 2 STR points
Agility (AGI) Increases Finesse/Ranged Damage (Daggers, Bows) +0.1% Evasion & +0.1% Critical Chance per AGI
Intelligence (INT) Increases Magic Damage (Wands, Staffs, Spells) +5 Max Mana per INT point (Weavers) & Elemental Resists
Stamina (STA) Core Survival Attribute +10 Maximum HP per STA point & +1 Armor per 4 STA

Elemental Infusions & Combat Utility Caps

Combat Stat Type Hard Cap Description & Synergy

⚡ Glitched Modifiers & The Level 70 Echo Quest

Progression beyond Level 70 is unlocked via the pinnacle quest "Breaking the Core". Completing it grants access to Glitched (Red) item drops and Paragon-style Echo micro-allocation.

Corrupted Item Infusion
Post-Echo Pinnacle Mechanics
System Rule

Once "Breaking the Core" is cleared, all newly dropped Legendary and Set items have a 10% baseline probability to roll a Glitched Modifier on top of their normal modifier budget.

  • Positive Effect: Massive god-tier buffs (+250% skill dmg, double casts, etc.).
  • Negative Penalty: Severe build constraints (-50% Max Mana, zero evasion, HP recoil).
The Echo Point System
Infinite Level 70+ Progression
Paragon

EXP earned after Level 70 fills the Echo Bar. Each Echo Level grants 1 point for micro-optimizations:

  • Offense: +0.2% Crit Damage, +0.1% Attack Speed, +0.2% Elemental Infusion.
  • Defense: +0.2% Armor, +0.1% Life Steal, +0.2% All-Resist.
  • Utility: +0.5% Gold Drop Rate, +0.5% Magic Find, +0.2% Resource Generation.
