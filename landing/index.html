Mechanics & Architecture

Deep ARPG Systems, Pure Text Immersion

Built with a reactive clean architecture. No artificial microtransactions, no mandatory cloud connectivity.

💾

Offline-First Determinism

All game progression, dynamic loot instances, room states, and bestiary records persist inside an offline SQLite database via Drift. Play anywhere with zero latency.

💎

Diablo-Style Loot & Sockets

Procedural drops across 5 rarity tiers: Normal, Magic, Rare, Legendary, Set. Roll custom prefixes, suffixes, and slot Chipped-to-Perfect gems to tailor your build.

⚔️

Tactical Turn-Based Combat

Dynamic initiative order, accuracy vs evasion checks, condition damage (Bleed, Shock, Glitch), and tactical skill choices powered by our pure Dart combat engine.

🛡️

Branching Class Evolutions

Start as a humble Vagabond. At Lv 20, choose your path: Juggernaut, Phantom, Weaver, or Warden. Master specialized Lv 50 Ascensions like Blood Mage or Void-Knight.

🗺️

Node & Grid Exploration

Traverse 5 expansive Acts with 5-6 major Nodes each containing 20-50 interconnected rooms with cardinal directions, hidden caches, and roaming bosses.

🐾

Companions & Echo Mastery

Equip mercenaries for frontline pressure and summon utility pets for auto-salvaging. Push beyond level cap with the post-cap Echo Point progression system.
