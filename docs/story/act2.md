## Act 2: The Sunken City

**Global Status:** *[System Notice: Depth limit exceeded. Compensating pressure via Void-Shielding. Warning: Liquid Data detected.]*

**Theme:** A sprawling, ancient metropolis that has collapsed into a trench of liquid Void—a heavy, suffocating substance that acts like water but glows with corrupted data streams. The architecture is a mix of grand gothic spires and rusted industrial pipes, illuminated by flickering, glitching neon signs.

*Objective: You have entered a high-pressure zone. Navigate the flooded outskirts and find a stabilized air pocket.*

* **Room 2101: The Arrival Plate (Spawn Point)**
* **Text:** You step out of the shimmering portal. The air is instantly heavy, tasting of copper and ozone. You are standing on a fractured stone plaza slowly sinking into a sea of murky, bioluminescent blue fluid. Above you, the "sky" is the underside of an ocean of fog.

* **Room 2102: The Coral Streets**
* **Text:** You wade through knee-high liquid Void. The stone buildings around you are overgrown not with seaweed, but with branching fiber-optic coral that pulses with raw data.


* **Room 2103: The Toll Bridge**
* **Text:** A collapsed bridge blocks your path. Guarding the ruin is a bloated corpse in rusted city-guard armor. Water pours continuously from the visor of its helmet, defying gravity to float upward into the fog. *[Combat Encounter: Drowned Enforcer]*

* **Text:** You reach a massive circular vault door. The locking mechanism is guarded by a swarm of mechanical crabs that scavenge bits of code from the environment.
* **Boss:** The Scavenger Swarm. (Drops: *Access Keycard - Beta* and Magic Rarity Gloves).



### Node 2: The Neon Slums (Safe Hub)

**Quest Updated: [A Breath of Code]**
*Objective: The local survivors have rigged a sanctuary using old neon signage to repel the liquid Void. Speak to their leader.*

* **Room 2201: The Air-Lock**
* **Text:** You insert the Keycard. The heavy vault door grinds open, and a pressurized wave of dry air hits you. The liquid Void is held back by a shimmering energy field.


* **Room 2202: The Neon Plaza**
* **Text:** A shanty town built inside a hollowed-out cathedral. The area is illuminated by dozens of flickering, holographic neon signs scavenged from the Severance. Survivors wear diving suits patched with leather and steel.

* **Room 2203: The Scavenger's Den**
* **Text:** A merchant named **Rook** sits atop a pile of rusted gears and glowing memory drives. He has gills surgically grafted to his neck. "Drylander," he hisses. "Got coins? I've got salvage. Best weapons this side of the crush-depth." *(Unlocks Act 2 Shop & Higher Tier Gear).*


* **Room 2204: The Data-Pool**
* **Text:** A serene, glowing pool of stabilized Void water. **Oracle Lin**, a blind woman with glowing digital eyes, meditates here. She can refine your skill paths and heal your wounds for a price. *(Unlocks Job Change Board for Level 20).*



### Node 3: The Submerged Archives (Dungeon)

**Quest Started: [Drowning in Knowledge]**
*Objective: Rook needs the blueprints to the city's main pumping station. They are buried in the flooded library.*

* **Room 2301: The Flooded Atrium**
* **Text:** You enter the grand library. The liquid Void is waist-deep here. Thousands of rotting books float in the water, their ink bleeding out to form floating, glowing sentences in the air.


* **Room 2302: The Aisle of Whispers**
* **Text:** Navigating the towering bookshelves, the floating text begins to swarm. The loose data forms into humanoid shapes—ghosts made entirely of rapidly scrolling text. *[Combat: 3x Glitch-Wraiths]*


* **Room 2303: The Reading Room (Side Room)**
* **Text:** A dry alcove protected by a localized forcefield. You find an ancient terminal. *[Loot: Rare Rarity Helmet with +Water Resistance, 2x Flawed Sapphires]*


* **Room 2304: The Server-Tomb (Boss Room)**
* **Text:** At the center of the archives is a massive mainframe wrapped in rusted chains and human bones. The machine awakens, its optical drive glowing like a furious red eye. Cables snake through the water like tentacles.
* **Boss:** The Archivist.
* **Mechanic:** Periodically casts "Data Flood," lowering your Evasion to zero for 2 turns. (Drops: *Pumping Station Blueprints* and a guaranteed Legendary Weapon).

### Node 5: The Abyssal Spire (Act 2 Finale)

**Quest Started: [Core Dump]**
*Objective: You have reached the trench floor. The Echo controlling the Sunken City lurks in the absolute dark. End its reign.*

* **Room 2501: The Trench Edge**
* **Text:** You use the Abyssal Key to open the floor hatch and drop into the deepest part of the city. It is pitch black. The only light comes from the bioluminescence of your own equipped gear.


* **Room 2502: The Crush Depth**
* **Text:** The pressure is agonizing. Your UI glitches violently, the text shifting colors. Gigantic, shadowy leviathans swim in the background, just out of bounds. *[Combat: 2x Deep-Sea Phantoms]*


* **Room 2503: The Throne of Data (Act 2 Boss Room)**
* **Text:** You step into a massive, sunken amphitheater. Sitting on a throne of jagged glass and server racks is a towering figure. He wears a diving suit of ornate brass, but the faceplate is shattered, revealing a vortex of swirling blue code. He is the Drowned King, an Echo of a system administrator who tried to save this sector and failed.
* **Boss:** The Drowned King.
* **Mechanic:** At 30% HP, he casts "System Override"—he steals one of your equipped item's modifiers (e.g., your +Strength) and applies it to himself for the remainder of the fight.


* **Room 2504: The Ascendancy (Act Complete)**
* **Text:** The Drowned King collapses, his brass armor dissolving into harmless code. The heavy liquid Void in the room instantly drains, shooting upward in a massive geyser of light, revealing a dry stone staircase leading higher into the fog.
