## Act 3: The Clockwork Peaks

**Global Status:** *[System Notice: Altitude warning. Temperature dropping below operational parameters. System clock sync errors detected. Time dilation in effect.]*

**Theme:** The ascent out of the liquid Void reveals that the "mountain" is actually a colossal, malfunctioning clockwork engine. Jagged stone is interwoven with continent-sized brass gears, pistons, and glowing frost. The enemies here are corrupted automatons and frozen time-anomalies.

**Quest Started: [The Frozen Climb]**
*Objective: Scale the exterior gears of the mountain. Survive the freezing altitude and find the internal sanctuary.*

* **Room 3101: The Geyser Plateau (Spawn Point)**
* **Text:** You emerge from the Ascendancy staircase into blinding, freezing wind. You are standing on a plateau of solid brass, high above the ocean of fog. The mountain before you grinds with the deafening sound of a million turning gears. Steam erupts from vents, instantly freezing into digital snowflakes.

* **Room 3102: The Wind-Torn Ridge**
* **Text:** You inch along a narrow path carved into the side of a spinning cog. The wind howls, carrying the shattered remnants of old system logs. A creature made of jagged ice and rusted wire swoops down from the blinding whiteout. *[Combat Encounter: Frost-Bitten Harpy]*


* **Room 3103: The Grinding Fault**
* **Text:** Two massive gears meet here, grinding together with earth-shattering force. To cross, you must time your jump perfectly. A mechanical beast, shaped like a wolf but built from discarded sawblades, guards the gap.
* **Mini-Boss:** The Gear-Hound. (Drops: *Spiked Cleats* - required to traverse the ice).

**Quest Updated: [The Clockmaker's Haven]**

* **Room 3201: The Brass Gates**
* **Text:** Embedded in the side of a stationary, frozen gear is a heavy steel door glowing with warm, orange firelight. You push it open and step out of the lethal wind.


* **Room 3202: The Grand Escapement**
* **Text:** The interior is a massive, hollowed-out cavern filled with ticking pendulums and warm hearths. The survivors here tick and whir as they move, their bodies modified with brass and glowing data-tubes to survive the cold.

* **Room 3203: The Tinker's Shop**
* **Text:** **Silas the Tinker**, a man whose lower jaw is a metal grill that vents steam, inspects your gear. "You're fighting with primitive scrap, Vagabond. Let me wire some kinetic energy into that." *(Unlocks Gear Upgrading: Reroll item modifiers).*


* **Room 3204: The Chrono-Clinic**
* **Text:** **Doctor Vane** operates in a sterile room lit by ultraviolet neon. Her hands move in a blur, literally skipping frames of time to perform surgeries faster. "Your soul is fraying from the Void jumps. Sit. Let me anchor you." *(Unlocks Level 50 Job Awakening Board).*



### Node 3: The Frozen Boiler (Dungeon)

**Quest Started: [Thawing the Core]**
*Objective: The Refuge is running out of heat. The mountain's primary boiler room has been overrun by frost-corrupted elementals. Reignite the furnace.*

* **Room 3301: The Iced Pipes**
* **Text:** You descend into the lower engine decks. The massive pipes that should be pumping steam are coated in thick, glowing blue ice. The air is so cold it burns your lungs. `[WARNING: CORE TEMP CRITICAL. REDUCED AGILITY.]`


* **Room 3302: The Furnace Room**
* **Text:** The central hearth is completely extinguished. Beings of corrupted digital fire, their flames turned a freezing, pixelated blue, wander the room desperately trying to consume any remaining warmth. *[Combat: 2x Glitch-Frost Elementals]*


* **Room 3303: The Engineer's Quarters (Side Room)**
* **Text:** A small, frozen office. The chief engineer's skeleton sits at a desk, a heavy wrench still gripped in his hands. *[Loot: Rare Rarity 2-Handed Mace "The Piston Pounder", 3x Flawless Topaz]*


* **Room 3304: The Boiler Heart (Boss Room)**
* **Text:** Encased in a massive block of black ice is the boiler's core mechanism. Guarding it is a towering suit of brass power-armor, possessed by the freezing Void energy.
* **Boss:** The Frost-Forged Warden.
* **Mechanic:** Casts "Absolute Zero." If the fight lasts longer than 8 turns, the player is instantly frozen and takes massive unavoidable damage. (Drops: *The Ignition Core*).



### Node 4: The Pendulum Chasm (Dungeon)

**Quest Started: [Syncing the System]**
*Objective: The mountain's clock is out of sync, causing reality to stutter. Traverse the great chasm and repair the master spring.*

* **Room 3401: The Ticking Bridge**
* **Text:** A bottomless chasm spans before you. The only way across is a series of massive, swinging pendulums. The ticking sound echoes like thunder.


* **Room 3402: The Time-Slip Gallery**
* **Text:** The architecture here is broken. A staircase loops back on itself in a physical paradox. Shadowy figures flicker in and out of existence—echoes of travelers who fell and are trapped in a time loop. *[Combat: 3x Chrono-Phantoms]*


* **Room 3403: The Master Spring (Boss Room)**
* **Text:** At the center of the chasm is a colossal, tightly coiled spring. Hovering above it is a creature composed entirely of shattered clock faces and glowing geometric light.
* **Image Placeholder:** `[Image_Prompt: A surreal, dark room. A massive coiled metal spring serves as the floor. Hovering above is a terrifying entity made of shattered clock faces and glowing digital light.]`

### Node 5: The Apex Gear (Act 3 Finale)

**Quest Started: [Overclocking]**
*Objective: Reach the absolute summit of the Clockwork Peaks. Confront the Echo of the Architect and re-sync the mountain to the realm.*

* **Room 3501: The Summit Point**
* **Text:** You breach the surface of the mountain. You are so high up that the fog is gone. Instead, the sky is a shattered glass dome, revealing a terrifying view of pure, rushing source code in the dark cosmos beyond.


* **Room 3502: The Hands of Fate**
* **Text:** The floor of the summit is the face of a clock the size of a city. You must walk across the minute hand to reach the center. The wind threatens to throw you into the digital abyss below.


* **Room 3503: The Chrono-Core (Act 3 Boss Room)**
* **Text:** Floating at the center axis of the clock face is a pristine, ivory-white android. Half of its face is torn away, revealing bloody, human tissue beneath the wireframe—a horrifying fusion of creator and machine. He is the Echo of the Architect.
* **Boss:** The Grand Automaton.
* **Mechanic:** "System Halt." Every 4th turn, the player loses their turn entirely, and the Automaton gets a guaranteed critical strike.


* **Room 3504: The Synchronization (Act Complete)**
* **Text:** The Automaton falls, its chassis shattering into thousands of glass gears. The massive clock face beneath your feet groans, aligns, and finally begins to tick in perfect rhythm. A beam of blinding light shoots from the center axis, piercing the digital sky-dome and creating a pathway upward.
