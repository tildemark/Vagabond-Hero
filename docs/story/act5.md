## Act 5: The Unallocated Space (New Game+)

**Global Status:** *[ANOMALY DETECTED. Unregistered User found in Clean Build. Initiating Quarantine and Purge.]*

**Theme:** Act 4 ended with the system rebooting into a beautiful, repaired world. Act 5 shatters that illusion. The system recognizes you as a foreign entity—a virus—and drops you out of the rendered world into the "Unallocated Space," the digital wasteland where deleted files, deprecated assets, and malicious code are quarantined. The environment is stark: wireframe geometry, absolute darkness, and chaotic, mashed-together assets.

### Node 1: The Simulation Edge (Entry Area)

**Quest Started: [Out of Bounds]**
*Objective: The repaired world of Oakhaven just dissolved beneath your feet. Survive the fall into the system's trash sector.*

* **Room 5101: The Render Drop (Spawn Point)**
* **Text:** For a brief moment, you felt the sun of a restored Oakhaven. Then, the sky rendered into a harsh red error screen, and the ground simply vanished. You fall through a void of infinite black, landing heavily on a surface of glowing green grid lines. Above you, you can see the "bottom" of the game world—a flat, untextured ceiling. You have been thrown out of bounds.
* **Image Placeholder:** `[Image_Prompt: A stark, dark cyberpunk void. The ground is a glowing neon green wireframe grid. Above, massive flat geometric shapes form the 'underside' of a rendered 3D world. Lonely, cosmic atmosphere.]`


* **Room 5102: The Dropped Packets**
* **Text:** Piles of discarded digital junk litter the grid. You recognize a sword from Act 1 fused seamlessly into the stone head of an Act 2 gargoyle. The junk twitches.


* **Room 5103: The Chimera Glitch**
* **Text:** The pile of assets collapses together, forming a monstrous, unsymmetrical beast. It has the legs of a Void-Rat, the torso of a Cultist, and a gear for a head. *[Combat Encounter: 2x Asset-Chimeras]*


* **Room 5104: The Firewall Breach (Mini-Boss Room)**
* **Text:** A massive, jagged hole is burned through a wall of static. Guarding the breach is a massive, pixelated hounds-tooth pattern that shifts and roars like a lion.
* **Boss:** The MissingNo. (Drops: *Quarantine Pass* and a glitched consumable).



### Node 2: The Quarantine Sector (Safe Hub)

**Quest Updated: [The Deleted Ones]**
*Objective: You aren't the only thing the system threw away. Find the settlement of deprecated NPCs.*

* **Room 5201: The Sandbox**
* **Text:** You step through the static wall into a grey, untextured room. This is a testing sandbox the developers abandoned. Camped out among floating grey cubes are NPCs whose code was deemed too unstable for the final release.
* **Image Placeholder:** `[Image_Prompt: A completely grey, untextured 3D environment with floating cubes. Camped among the cubes are bizarre, half-rendered fantasy characters with missing limbs or T-posing glitches. Eerie but peaceful.]`


* **Room 5202: The Beta-Merchant**
* **Text:** **Merchant_ID_00** stands stuck in a permanent T-pose, communicating purely through system notifications. "BUY_MENU_INITIALIZED. I HAVE ILLEGAL EXCEPTIONS." *(Unlocks New Game+ Gear: 'Glitched' Rarity items that offer massive stats but severe negative debuffs).*


* **Room 5203: The Cut Content**
* **Text:** A heavily armored knight sits without a helmet. His head is a low-resolution grey box. "I was supposed to be the Act 1 Boss," he laments. "Now I upgrade gear." *(Unlocks Tier 5 Crafting: Fusing Legendary items together).*


* **Room 5204: The Debug Console**
* **Text:** A massive, floating green command prompt. By entering the right hexadecimal codes found in Act 5, you can permanently alter the game's difficulty modifiers for better loot drops. *(Unlocks the Mutator System).*



### Node 3: The Fragmented Memory (Dungeon)

**Quest Started: [Stack Overflow]**
*Objective: The only way out of Quarantine is to overload the sector's memory cache. Travel through the fragmented archives.*

* **Room 5301: The Endless Hallway**
* **Text:** The physics engine is broken here. The hallway loops infinitely if you look backward, but stretches normally if you walk forward. Textures warp and smear like wet paint.


* **Room 5302: The Recursive Loop**
* **Text:** Swarms of insectoid machines made of brackets `[]` and semicolons `;` devour chunks of the floor. *[Combat Encounter: 3x Syntax-Crawlers]*


* **Room 5303: The Overflow Buffer (Side Room)**
* **Text:** A room where the gravity is reversed. You walk on the ceiling to open a chest that is clipping through a wall. *[Loot: Glitched Rarity Weapon: 'The Null Pointer']*


* **Room 5304: The Infinite Loop (Boss Room)**
* **Text:** A massive, serpentine dragon made entirely of cascading green code spirals endlessly around itself, eating its own tail. It is a runaway script threatening to crash the sector.
* **Boss:** The Ouroboros Script.
* **Mechanic:** "Stack Overflow"—Every time you hit the boss, it summons a `Child Process` minion. If there are more than 4 minions on the field, the sector crashes (instant wipe). You must alternate between single-target damage on the boss and AoE clears to manage the stack. (Drops: *Corrupted Memory Drive*).



### Node 4: The Dead-Code Wastes (Dungeon)

**Quest Started: [Garbage Collection]**
*Objective: The system's automated cleaner is sweeping the wastes. Destroy it to halt the purge of the Quarantine Sector.*

* **Room 5401: The Formatted Plains**
* **Text:** A vast, flat plain of blinding white. Nothing exists here except the occasional cluster of corrupted data. A massive, deafening hum vibrates through the "ground."


* **Room 5402: The Sweep**
* **Text:** A wall of pixelated erasure moves slowly across the horizon, deleting everything it touches. Panic-stricken glitches flee before it. *[Combat Encounter: 2x Corrupted Vagabonds (Echoes of dead players)]*


* **Room 5403: The Collector's Maw (Boss Room)**
* **Text:** The source of the erasure is a colossal, blocky machine resembling a bulldozer fused with a black hole. It consumes code and leaves absolute null space in its wake.
* **Image Placeholder:** `[Image_Prompt: A massive, terrifying geometric machine, resembling a brutalist bulldozer, slowly crawling across a white void. It sucks up glowing data, leaving a trail of absolute black nothingness behind it.]`
* **Boss:** The Garbage Collector.
* **Mechanic:** "Null Space"—Every 2 turns, the boss permanently deletes one of your combat UI buttons at random (e.g., your Potion button, then your Skill 2 button). You must kill it before it deletes your 'Attack' command. (Drops: *The Admin Credentials*).



### Node 5: The Master Boot Record (Act 5 Finale)

**Quest Started: [Force Quit]**
*Objective: Use the Admin Credentials to reach the deepest level of the hardware. Break the system's lock and write yourself into the core architecture.*

* **Room 5501: The Silicon Descent**
* **Text:** You insert the Admin Credentials into a floating terminal. A trapdoor opens, dropping you into a shaft of pure silicon and copper wiring.


* **Room 5502: The Logic Gates**
* **Text:** Massive electrical currents surge through the pathways. You must defeat the core security daemons before the gates open. *[Combat: 3x Elite Logic-Daemons]*


* **Room 5503: The Kernel Panic (Final Boss Room)**
* **Text:** You reach the literal heart of the machine. It isn't a program; it's a terrifying, chaotic storm of raw, uncompiled energy that constantly shifts between the faces of every boss you've ever fought. The system is violently rejecting your presence.
* **Boss:** The Kernel Panic.
* **Mechanic:** "Phase Shift"—Every turn, the boss changes its elemental affinity and damage type.
* **Mechanic (At 10% HP):** "Blue Screen of Death"—The text log fills with hexadecimal error codes. You have exactly one turn to deal a massive finishing blow. If you fail, the app literally forces a crash-to-desktop/home screen (the ultimate meta-wipe).


* **Room 5504: The Write Permission (True Ending)**
* **Text:** The storm collapses. The hex codes fade.
* A simple, blinking cursor remains in the center of the void.
* `> UNREGISTERED ENTITY RECOGNIZED.`
* `> ASSIGNING ROOT PRIVILEGES...`
* `> WELCOME, ADMIN_VAGABOND.`
* You have not just survived the game; you now own it. The Shattered Expanse is yours to reshape.
