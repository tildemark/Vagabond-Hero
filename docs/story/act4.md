## Act 4: The Astral Servers

**Global Status:** *[CRITICAL ALERT: Root directory breached. Reality anchor failing. Initiating protocol: OMEGA-WIPE. Warning: Soul deletion imminent.]*

**Theme:** The absolute core of the Shattered Expanse. The illusion of a fantasy world drops entirely. You are walking through the physical manifestation of the game's server architecture—a breathtaking, terrifying expanse of floating monoliths, rivers of glowing liquid data, and cosmic void. The enemies here are "Biblically accurate" AI programs, security daemons, and manifested code.

### Node 1: The Fiber-Optic Bridge (Entry Area)

**Quest Started: [Piercing the Veil]**
*Objective: The Clockwork Peaks deposited you at the edge of the system boundary. Cross the data-stream into the mainframe.*

* **Room 4101: The Edge of the Render (Spawn Point)**
* **Text:** You step off the highest gear of the Clockwork Peaks and into... nothing. The sky, the weather, the ground—it all vanishes. You are standing on a bridge of pure, braided fiber-optic light suspended in a starry, infinite void. Massive, black rectangular monoliths (server racks) drift in the distance, pulsing with rhythmic LED constellations.
* **Image Placeholder:** `[Image_Prompt: A cosmic void where massive, imposing server racks float like monoliths in space. A bridge of glowing fiber-optic cables leads toward a blindingly bright central processor. Cyberpunk meets cosmic horror.]`


* **Room 4102: The Bandwidth Stream**
* **Text:** The bridge hums beneath your feet. Ghostly data packets—memories of millions of players who used to inhabit this world—flash past you at lightspeed.


* **Room 4103: The Packet Sniffers**
* **Text:** The bridge security awakens. Geometric shapes of spinning, razor-sharp glass descend from the void, scanning your Vagabond signature. `[UNAUTHORIZED ENTITY. COMMENCING DELETION.]` *[Combat Encounter: 3x Glass-Daemons]*


* **Room 4104: The MAC Address (Mini-Boss Room)**
* **Text:** A massive gate of hard-light blocks the path to the Hub. Guarding it is a centaur-like construct made of chrome and liquid mercury, wielding a halberd of pure plasma.
* **Boss:** The Gate-Daemon. (Drops: *Root Access Key* and a Flawless Amethyst).



### Node 2: The Root Directory (Safe Hub)

**Quest Updated: [The Last Users]**
*Objective: The Root Access Key parts the hard-light gate. Enter the final sanctuary and prepare for the core.*

* **Room 4201: The Kernel**
* **Text:** You enter a massive, hollowed-out cooling tower. This is the last safe haven in the Shattered Expanse. It isn't populated by survivors, but by rogue admin programs and the digital ghosts of the original developers. The air is sterile, and the ambient music is a slow, mournful synth drone.
* **Image Placeholder:** `[Image_Prompt: A sterile, futuristic sanctuary inside a massive server cooling tower. Holographic displays float in the air. Figures in sleek, glowing cybernetic suits sit around a central terminal. Clean, stark, high-tech aesthetics.]`


* **Room 4202: The Compiler's Forge**
* **Text:** **Hephaestus-01**, an AI intelligence housed in a hovering, heavy-loader mech suit, monitors a forge of pure plasma. "Your code is fragmented, Vagabond. Let me compile your final loadout." *(Unlocks Tier 4 Crafting & Perfect Gems. Allows re-rolling of Legendary Item traits).*


* **Room 4203: The Archive Merchant**
* **Text:** **The Last User** sits at a glowing terminal. They look exactly like a modern-day human in a hoodie, their face obscured by monitor glare. "I got trapped here during the Severance too," they say. "Take my stash. I'm not making it out." *(Unlocks Endgame Consumables: Full Restore Potions, Resurrection Protocols).*


* **Room 4204: The Command Prompt**
* **Text:** A solitary, glowing keyboard floating over a bottomless shaft. Here, you can respec your entire build, reallocate your Job classes, and prepare your final strategy.



### Node 3: The Corrupted Cache (Dungeon)

**Quest Started: [Memory Leak]**
*Objective: To reach the CPU, you must bypass the Memory Banks. The area is heavily corrupted by the Void, warping past assets into nightmares.*

* **Room 4301: The Sector Graveyard**
* **Text:** You enter a vast plain of shattered glass drives. Discarded assets from Acts 1, 2, and 3 glitch in and out of reality—a half-rendered tree, a floating piece of neon sign, a frozen gear.


* **Room 4302: The Fragmentation**
* **Text:** The corrupted memory coalesces. Amorphous blobs of static and missing textures crawl toward you, absorbing the environment. *[Combat Encounter: 4x Static-Slimes]*


* **Room 4303: The Quarantine Zone (Side Room)**
* **Text:** You bypass a heavily encrypted door using a sequence from your own world's logic. Inside is a pristine, untouched treasure cache. *[Loot: 1x Perfect Diamond, Rare Rarity Ring with +All Stats]*


* **Room 4304: The Garbage Collector (Boss Room)**
* **Text:** A massive, terrifying machine that looks like a hydraulic press fused with a metal spider. It is designed to delete corrupted data, and it has categorized you as a fatal error.
* **Boss:** The Memory Harvester.
* **Mechanic:** "Inventory Lock"—Every 3 turns, the boss disables your ability to use Potions or Consumables for 1 turn. You must rely purely on your base stats and life-steal during this window. (Drops: *The Override Code*).



### Node 4: The Great Firewall (Dungeon)

**Quest Started: [Breaching the Defenses]**
*Objective: The final barrier before the Motherboard. It is a literal wall of digital fire that burns code and flesh alike.*

* **Room 4401: The Burning Ports**
* **Text:** A monolithic wall stretches infinitely upward and downward. Massive ports vent jets of searing red plasma. You must navigate the narrow maintenance catwalks between the exhaust vents.


* **Room 4402: The Encryption Grid**
* **Text:** The catwalk transforms into a grid of laser-light. Seraphim made of golden code float down from the vents. They have rings of eyes and wield swords of burning logic. *[Combat Encounter: 2x Firewall Seraphs]*


* **Room 4403: The Bastion (Boss Room)**
* **Text:** The gate to the CPU is guarded by a towering knight in blinding white armor. He does not speak, but a system prompt fills your screen: `[EXECUTING QUARANTINE PROTOCOL. NO EXCEPTIONS.]`
* **Boss:** The Sentinel Program.
* **Mechanic:** "Aegis Shield"—The boss starts with an invulnerability shield and summons two Shield-Biters. You deal 0 damage to the Sentinel until both minions are destroyed. Once destroyed, you have a 3-turn window to deal damage before the shield recharges. (Drops: *The Final Key*).



### Node 5: The Motherboard (Act 4 Finale / Base Game End)

**Quest Started: [Task Manager]**
*Objective: You have reached the core of the Shattered Expanse. End the Severance. Log off.*

* **Room 4501: The Socket**
* **Text:** You use The Final Key. The Firewall parts, revealing a blindingly white corridor. The ambient noise fades to absolute zero. Your UI starts to glitch, elements of the HUD disappearing and reappearing.


* **Room 4502: The Processing Lanes**
* **Text:** You walk down a massive corridor lined with golden circuitry. Shadows of your past battles replay in the reflections of the floor. There are no enemies here. Just the heavy, terrifying weight of the system's core processing power.


* **Room 4503: The Null Point (Final Boss Room)**
* **Text:** You reach a circular platform suspended in a sea of white light. At the center is a massive, shifting geometric shape—a tesseract folding in on itself. From it emerges a humanoid figure made of pure, blinding code. It wears the face of the Vagabond from your character sheet. It is the Prime Override, the manifestation of the game's core engine, gone rogue.
* **Image Placeholder:** `[Image_Prompt: A blindingly white, ethereal arena. In the center floats a terrifying, majestic entity made of folding geometric shapes and glowing white code, projecting a god-like aura of digital power.]`
* **Boss:** The Prime Override.
* **Mechanic Phase 1:** "Source Code"—The boss mirrors your exact base stats.
* **Mechanic Phase 2 (at 50% HP):** "Fatal Exception"—The boss begins a 4-turn countdown. The text log flashes red: `[INITIATING OMEGA-WIPE. 4 TURNS UNTIL DELETION.]` If you do not reduce its HP to 0 before the 4th turn, it triggers an instant game-over, bypassing all HP and defense.


* **Room 4504: The Reboot (Epilogue)**
* **Text:** The Prime Override shatters. The blinding light recedes.
* A massive, blinking command prompt appears in the center of your screen:
* `> SHATTERED_EXPANSE.EXE HAS STOPPED RESPONDING.`
* `> REBOOTING SYSTEM IN SAFE MODE...`
* `> VAGABOND SOUL_DATA SAVED.`
* `> CONNECTION SEVERED.`
* The digital fog dissolves into a clear, blue sky. The world of Oakhaven begins to render properly around you, lush and whole. You didn't just survive the game; you fixed it.
* `[CONGRATULATIONS. BASE GAME COMPLETE. NEW GAME+ UNLOCKED.]`
