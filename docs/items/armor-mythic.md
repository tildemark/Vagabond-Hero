## Mythic Rarity Budget & Rules

In accordance with `docs/item-mechanics.md`:
* **Rarity:** Mythic (Orange)
* **Modifier Structure:** Elevated High Base Stat (Armor / Defense) + 1 High Permanent Thematic Modifier (Does Not Change) + 1 High Core Attribute + 1 High Random Class Affix + 1 Game-Changing Signature Trait.
* **Class Weighting:** Specifically forged for each class archetype:
  * **Vagabond:** Universal survivor gear (Levels 10–20), scrap technology, survival mechanics, high luck/gold/exp.
  * **Juggernaut:** Strength & Stamina, maximum armor, damage reflect/thorns, block chance, physical damage mitigation.
  * **Phantom:** Agility, extreme critical damage/chance, evasion, stealth, attack speed, poison enhancement.
  * **Weaver:** Intelligence, mana scaling, cast speed, elemental damage, magic penetration, cooldown reduction.
  * **Warden:** Hybrid Str/Int & Stamina, pet/summon synergy, thorns/life steal, nature/void communion, shapeshifting.
